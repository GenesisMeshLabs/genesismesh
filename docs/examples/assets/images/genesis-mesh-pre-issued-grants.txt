==> 1. The policy and the device's key
$ genesis-mesh na start --env-file na.env
!dim   EVIDENCE_STORE=on  EVIDENCE_OUT_OF_BAND=on  EVIDENCE_GRANTS=on
$ POST /admin/boundary-policies  (secret-lifetime: lifetime_days <= 90), then activate
!ok 200: secret-lifetime v1 active
$ POST /admin/evidence/executor-keys {"key_id": "site-7-device", ...}
!ok 201: device key registered
==> 2. The grant: four hours, three uses, lifetimes 1 to 60 days
$ POST /admin/grants  (lifetime_days 1..60, max_uses 3, max_uses_per_time_token 2)
!ok 201: grant 39ef4dd4..., bound to secret-lifetime v1 (max-lifetime=pass)
$ POST /admin/grants  (lifetime_days 1..400)
!no 422 grant_gate_may_deny
==> 3. Offline: two rotations, signed by the device
  use 1: rotate kv:site-7/api-key lifetime_days=30 version_id=v8
  use 2: rotate kv:site-7/api-key lifetime_days=30 version_id=v9
==> 4. Reconnecting
$ POST /grants/time-tokens  {"use_sequence": 2, "use_head_digest": "<second.digest()>"}
!ok 201: fresh time token, use_sequence 2
$ POST /evidence/grant-executions  (use 1)
!ok 201: resource_sequence 1
$ POST /evidence/grant-executions  (use 2)
!ok 201: resource_sequence 2
$ POST /evidence/grant-executions  (use 3, lifetime_days=400)
!no 422 grant_envelope_mismatch, kept as quarantine 7b0576ab...
$ GET /admin/grants/<grant_id>/uses
!no use 3  quarantined  grant_envelope_mismatch
  use 2  admitted
  use 1  admitted
==> 5. The observer agrees
$ POST /evidence/observations  (rotate v8)
!ok 201: governed_by=grant verdict=allow
$ POST /evidence/observations  (rotate v9)
!ok 201: governed_by=grant verdict=allow
==> 6. A stricter policy revokes the grant
$ POST /admin/boundary-policies  (tighter: lifetime_days <= 30), then activate
!ok 200: tighter v1 active
$ GET /admin/grants/<grant_id>
!no status=revoked reason=policy_activated
$ GET /sovereign-revocation-feed
  sequence=1 revoked_grant_ids=['39ef4dd4...']

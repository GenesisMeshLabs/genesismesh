# Core Trust & Sovereignty

Membership attestations, recognition treaties, cross-sovereign revocation
propagation, the independent-sovereigns proof, two sovereigns run from the
signed images, and the recognition graph as the Connectome and the Trust
Atlas. These are the foundational examples for how trust travels across
sovereign boundaries.

- {doc}`sovereign-attestations` — one sovereign issues a signed membership attestation; another accepts or rejects it
- {doc}`recognition-treaties` — signed, scoped and revocable recognition between two sovereigns
- {doc}`cross-sovereign-revocation` — signed revocation feeds that withdraw trust across a treaty
- {doc}`independent-sovereigns` — the recognition and revocation flow across two clouds
- {doc}`two-sovereigns-from-images` — two sovereigns from the published image on one host
- {doc}`connectome` — the recognition graph as an operator view, with trust paths and blast radius
- {doc}`atlas` — a read-only explorer over the recognition graph
